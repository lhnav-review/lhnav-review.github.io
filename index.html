Future Work

Brain responsiveness. Increasing the Brain’s effective update rate while reducing inference latency could enable more timely responses to environmental changes and user instructions.

Scene-aware locomotion. The current policy does not explicitly control the approach style near a destination: the robot may walk forward or move sideways. Future work will explore scene-aware motion generation with explicit control over approach direction and gait selection, adapting locomotion to surrounding geometry and the desired terminal pose.

Scalable scene preparation. Assisted annotation of traversable regions, target bounding boxes, and approach directions could reduce manual preparation effort and facilitate expansion of the data engine to additional environments.

Long-horizon navigation and manipulation. Our navigation–interaction demonstration illustrates the compatibility of simulated navigation data and real-robot manipulation data. Future work will extend the Brain’s task coordination to manipulation subtasks, integrating interaction feedback with navigation progress to support longer sequences of navigation and object interaction.
